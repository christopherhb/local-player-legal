Local Player privacy policy

Effective 24 August 2026

Local Player is a local video playback app developed by Christopher H B. This policy explains how Local Player handles information.

Data collection and sharing

Local Player does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data. The app has no Internet permission and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, account, or crash-reporting services.

Local files and on-device data

You choose videos through Android’s system document picker or Android’s Open with flow. Local Player accesses only the files that Android makes available to it. The app does not upload those files.

To support Recent and resume playback, the app stores the selected file reference, display name, and playback position on your device. This information remains in the app’s private storage and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

Local Player uses notification and foreground media playback capabilities to provide visible media controls and to continue user-initiated playback in picture-in-picture or while the screen is locked. It uses a wake lock only while required for active playback. It does not request broad storage access.

Data retention and deletion

Saved file references and playback positions remain on the device until you clear Local Player’s app storage or uninstall the app. Android settings can also be used to revoke file access. Local Player has no accounts and keeps no server-side data.

Children’s privacy

Local Player does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Local Player privacy support page.
